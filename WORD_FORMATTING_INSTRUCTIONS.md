# METHODOLOGY SECTION - FORMATTING GUIDE FOR MS WORD

## INSTRUCTIONS FOR APPLYING FORMATTING IN MS WORD:

### 1. HEADING STYLES

- **Main Heading (3. METHODOLOGY)**:
  - Font: Times New Roman, 14pt, Bold, Color: Dark Blue (#003366)
- **Section Headings (3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.4, 3.5)**:
  - Font: Times New Roman, 12pt, Bold, Color: Dark Blue (#003366)
- **Subsection Headings (3.2.1, 3.2.2, etc.)**:
  - Font: Times New Roman, 11pt, Bold, Color: Dark Blue (#003366)

- **Table Headers (Table 1: STUDENT, etc.)**:
  - Font: Times New Roman, 11pt, Bold
  - Background: Light Blue (#B8CCE4)
  - Text Color: Black

### 2. BODY TEXT

- Font: Times New Roman, 11pt, Black
- Line Spacing: 1.5
- Paragraph spacing: 6pt before, 6pt after

### 3. TABLE FORMATTING

- Header Row: Background Light Blue (#B8CCE4), Text Black, Bold
- Data Rows: Alternating Light Blue (#E7F0F7) and White
- Font: Times New Roman, 10pt
- Borders: 1pt Black

### 4. COLOR SCHEME (from your attachment)

- Header Background: Light Blue (#B8CCE4 or RGB 184, 204, 228)
- Alternating Row: Light Blue (#E7F0F7 or RGB 231, 240, 247)
- Text Headers: Dark Blue (#003366 or RGB 0, 51, 102)
- Regular Text: Black (#000000)

### 5. TABLE BORDERS

- All borders 1pt, Black solid line
- Header cells: Bold text, centered
