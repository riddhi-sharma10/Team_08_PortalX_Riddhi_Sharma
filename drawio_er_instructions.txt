QUICK SHAPE GUIDE (Keep this open while working in draw.io):
  [Rectangle]        = Entity (Table)
  (Single Oval)      = Regular Attribute / Column
  (Underlined Oval)  = Primary Key
  (Dashed Oval)      = Derived Attribute (calculated, not stored directly)
  ((Double Oval))    = Multi-Valued Attribute (can have multiple values)
  <Diamond>          = Relationship (verb connecting two entities)
  Line with "1"      = One side of the relationship
  Line with "N"      = Many side of the relationship
